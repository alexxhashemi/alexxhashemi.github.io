Projects

Things I have built, from AI systems to accessibility tools and VR.

Trustworthy AI

Multi-Agent Safety Case Generation for Physical AI

A group project at York University (GS/EECS 6467 Trustworthy AI Engineering). I built the Critic agent, which checks AI-drafted safety arguments against simulation failure logs, and I am leading the evaluation. It will lead to a paper; the manuscript is in progress.

Multi-agent system

Shiraz, a self-hosted multi-agent system

An orchestrator and six specialist agents that research, publish, and produce video with little supervision, with human approval where it matters. A working testbed for questions about agent autonomy, oversight, and failure. Architecture and design notes.

Accessibility tooling

Bulk VTT Validator

A React tool that checks Web Video Text Tracks files, speeding up audio description and closed caption verification and saving up to a month of validation time a year. Recognized with TMS's Professional Excellence and Innovation Award.

Virtual reality

The Bitter Truth

A VR life review experience exploring intrapersonal empathy and self-compassionate dialogue. Presented at ISEA 2025 in Seoul and exhibited at the 30th Toronto Reel Asian International Film Festival.

Mobile

Young Adult Peer Match

A mobile app connecting young adult cancer patients and survivors with peers navigating similar experiences. Featured in a radio interview on WGLT's Sound Health.

Serious games

Know Me Better

A mobile serious game enhancing social support for young adult cancer patients through empathy-driven mechanics, grounded in research published with Springer.

Virtual reality

Nightmare Virtual Gallery

A virtual reality experience exhibited at the Electronic Literature Organization (ELO) exhibition in Toronto.
